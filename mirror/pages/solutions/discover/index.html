FAQ

All You Need to Know

Quick answers to help you get the most out of your smart tools
What is the Discover service?

Discover helps you turn a technical or operational requirement into a clear set of suitable product and supplier options.

Can Dints help define our requirements?

Yes. We can review your specifications, budget, timeline and operating needs to clarify the procurement brief.

Do you work with specific brands?

Dints works across multiple brands and suppliers, allowing us to identify options based on suitability rather than a single manufacturer.

Can you find alternatives to a requested product?

Yes. Where appropriate, we can identify comparable options based on specification, availability, cost and lead time.

How do you select suppliers?

We consider factors such as capability, product quality, compliance, commercial terms and delivery requirements.

Can you support complex or unusual requirements?

Yes. Our team can research specialist products and coordinate requirements involving multiple suppliers, markets or technical specifications.

What information do you need to begin?

Typically, we need the product or equipment specification, quantity, destination, required timeline and any preferred brands or suppliers. We present the available options and can then support sourcing, financing and delivery through the next stages of the Dints process.
