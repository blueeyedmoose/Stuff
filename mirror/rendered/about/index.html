Geoffrey de Mowbray
Founder & CEO
gdem@dints.com

Founder of Dints and Chair of the British Exporters Association, Geoffrey has over two decades of experience building ecosystems across international trade, export finance and emerging markets.

His work has focused on connecting finance, supply chains and international delivery to help simplify complex cross-border trade and industrial projects. Outside of Dints, Geoffrey is passionate about aviation, regenerative agriculture and the study of samurai philosophy and martial arts.
