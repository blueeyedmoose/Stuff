FAQ

All You Need to Know

Quick answers to help you get the most out of your smart tools
What is Wealthis and how does it work?

We source equipment, spare parts and other operational requirements from OEMs, authorised dealers and approved suppliers.

Can you source from multiple brands?

Yes. We can combine requirements from multiple brands and suppliers through one managed process.

Do you supply genuine OEM parts?

Yes. We source genuine parts through verified OEMs and dealers, alongside approved alternatives where suitable.

Can you source alternative parts?

Yes. We can identify suitable alternatives based on quality, compatibility, availability and cost.

How do you verify suppliers?

Suppliers are assessed against relevant quality, compliance, regulatory and commercial requirements.

Can we use our preferred suppliers?

Yes. We can work with your existing suppliers or source additional options through our network.

Can you consolidate multiple orders?

Yes. Where practical, we can combine goods from different suppliers before shipment.
