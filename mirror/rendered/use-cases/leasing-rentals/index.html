FAQ

All You Need to Know

What does Dints do?

Dints helps businesses source, finance, procure and deliver equipment, parts and project requirements across markets and industries.

Who do you work with?

We support companies, contractors, dealers, distributors, OEM representatives and project owners with complex purchasing and financing needs.

Can you source from multiple brands and suppliers?

Yes. We can coordinate multi-brand and multi-supplier requirements through one managed process.

What types of finance are available?

Depending on the transaction, we can support equipment finance, leasing, rentals, inventory and floorplan facilities, and ECA-backed credit solutions.

Can you manage international delivery?

Yes. We can coordinate collection, consolidation, documentation, shipping and delivery across international markets.

Do you only supply new equipment?

No. Depending on the requirement, we can support new equipment, parts, rebuilds and refurbishment solutions.

Where does Dints operate?

Dints supports transactions and projects across multiple international markets, subject to supplier, financing, regulatory and logistical requirements.
