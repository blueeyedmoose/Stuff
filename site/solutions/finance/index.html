FAQ

All You Need to Know

Quick answers about arranging finance through Dints.
What can Dints help finance?

Depending on eligibility, we can support equipment, inventory, leasing, rentals and project-related purchases.

How much finance can you arrange?

Facility sizes vary according to the transaction, credit profile, jurisdiction and financial partner requirements.

What finance terms are available?

Terms are structured around the transaction and may include deposits, deferred payments, instalments or longer-term facilities.

Who provides the finance?

Facilities are provided in collaboration with banks, lenders, export credit agencies and other financial partners.

Is finance available in every country?

Availability depends on the borrower, transaction, jurisdiction and appetite of the relevant financial partners.

Is finance guaranteed?

No. All facilities remain subject to eligibility, due diligence, credit approval and final documentation.

What information is required?

This typically includes company details, financial statements, supplier quotations, transaction information and supporting documents.
